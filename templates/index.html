Road Accident Response
1
Ensure scene safety — turn on hazard lights, keep bystanders at safe distance.
2
Check consciousness — tap shoulder firmly and shout "Are you okay?"
3
Call 108 (ambulance) immediately. Do NOT move victim if spinal injury suspected.
4
Control visible bleeding with clean cloth — apply firm pressure for 10+ minutes.
5
Keep the victim warm and calm. Talk reassuringly until help arrives.
⚠️ Never remove helmet or move someone with suspected neck injury unless immediate life threat exists.
🩸
Severe Bleeding Control
1
Wear gloves if available. Apply direct pressure with clean cloth or bandage.
2
Do NOT remove the cloth if soaked — add more material on top.
3
Elevate the injured limb above heart level if possible.
4
For limb injuries, apply tourniquet 5cm above wound if bleeding is life-threatening.
⚠️ Tourniquet only as last resort. Write application time clearly on patient's skin.
💨
CPR — Unconscious / Not Breathing
1
Place on firm flat surface. Tilt head back, lift chin to open airway.
2
Check for breathing — 10 seconds max. If absent, begin CPR immediately.
3
30 chest compressions: Push hard and fast (100-120/min) on center of chest.
4
2 rescue breaths: Pinch nose, give 1-second breath, watch chest rise.
5
Repeat 30:2 cycle until ambulance arrives or AED available.
